\(\fb{\alpha} + \fc{\beta} + \gamma = 180^\circ\)
Parallele durch \(C\): Wechselwinkel
\(\fc{\beta'} = 180^\circ - \fc{\beta}\)
\(\fb{\alpha} + \gamma = 180^\circ - \fc{\beta}\)
\(\Rightarrow\ \fc{\beta'} = \fb{\alpha} + \gamma\)
\(\fb{\alpha} = 50^\circ, \quad \fc{\beta} = 60^\circ\)
\(\gamma = 180^\circ - 50^\circ - 60^\circ = 70^\circ\)
\(\fc{\beta'} = 180^\circ - 60^\circ = 120^\circ = 50^\circ + 70^\circ\)
