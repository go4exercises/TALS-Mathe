\(\sqrt[3]{x+2} = \fb{c}\)
Wo schneidet \(y = \fb{c}\) die Kurve?
\(\sqrt[3]{x+2} = \fb{1} \;\Rightarrow\; x = \fb{1}^3 - 2 = -1\)
Schnittpunkt \(P(-1 \mid \fb{1})\).
Probe: \(\sqrt[3]{-1+2} = \sqrt[3]{1} = 1\)
\(x = \fb{1.5}^3 - 2 = 3.375 - 2 = 1.375\)
Abgelesen \(x \approx 1.38\), gerechnet \(x = 1.375\).
\(\sqrt[3]{x+2} = \fb{2} \;\Rightarrow\; x = \fb{2}^3 - 2 = 6\)
Schnittpunkt \(P(6 \mid \fb{2})\).
Probe: \(\sqrt[3]{6+2} = \sqrt[3]{8} = 2\)
Die Lösung ist die \(x\)-Koordinate des Schnittpunkts.
\(\sqrt[3]{x+2} = \fb{c} \;\Rightarrow\; x = \fb{c}^3 - 2\)
