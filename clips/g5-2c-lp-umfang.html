\(\text{eine Umdrehung} = U\)
\(d\) passt gut dreimal in \(U\)
\(\pi = \dfrac{U}{d} \approx 3.1416\)
\(U = \pi d = 2\pi r\)
Sektoren abwechselnd nebeneinander
\(\text{Höhe } \fb{r}, \quad \text{Breite } \fc{\pi r}\)
\(A = \pi r \cdot r = \fc{\pi r^2}\)
\(r = 3\,\mathrm{cm}\)
\(U = 2\pi \cdot 3 = 6\pi \approx \fc{18.85\,\mathrm{cm}}\)
\(A = \pi \cdot 3^2 = 9\pi \approx \fc{28.27\,\mathrm{cm}^2}\)
\(U = 40\,\mathrm{cm}\!: \; r = \dfrac{40}{2\pi} \approx \fc{6.37\,\mathrm{cm}}\)
\(A = 50\,\mathrm{cm}^2\!: \; r = \sqrt{\dfrac{50}{\pi}} \approx \fc{3.99\,\mathrm{cm}}\)
